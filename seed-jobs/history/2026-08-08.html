1. NewCase.ai — AI Litigation Intelligence for Insurance Defense
newcase.ai · Wellfound
Problem & how it works: Insurance defense attorneys preparing for trial need to cross-reference an opposing expert witness's testimony across 50+ prior depositions to find contradictions. A single matter generates hundreds of transcript pages; manually reviewing the opposing expert's prior depositions takes 60+ hours and costs clients tens of thousands of dollars. Traditional legal databases (Westlaw, LexisNexis) don't help because they index public case law, not private deposition transcripts. NewCase ingests these private, unindexed transcripts and identifies pre-existing conditions, factual discrepancies, and expert witness contradictions, making reviews 15x faster. Example: an insurer defending a bodily-injury claim uploads 30 depositions from a plaintiff's expert; NewCase flags that the expert gave contradictory opinions on spinal injury causation in two prior cases. Customer: Insurance defense litigation teams — claims counsel, managing partners at defense firms. Why now: LLMs can now cross-reference free-text medical records and deposition transcripts at scale; previously this was keyword-search at best. 96% of matters settle, so this private data has never been searchable before. Revenue model: Per-seat or per-matter SaaS to law firms and insurers. The company focuses narrowly on expert witness reviews as a wedge into the $20B U.S. insurance defense industry.

Stage: Seed, founded 2024, based in Cambridge, MA. Raised $1.15M. ~2 employees. Not YC. COO Mustafa Awad is a dual-qualified lawyer and accountant with direct litigation workflow experience. Early paid pilot programs have demonstrated clear efficiency gains.
Bootstrap fit
Accumulates: A proprietary corpus of private deposition transcripts and expert-witness opinion graphs — each customer's uploads enrich the cross-reference graph, making contradiction detection stronger over time. No indie dev has this data on day one.
Ground truth: Yes — contradictions between two deposition statements are mechanically checkable (did Expert X say "causation is biomechanical" in Case A and "causation is psychological" in Case B?).
Proxy bootstrap: V1 can be built from publicly available sample depositions and court filings; eval set constructed by hand-labeling known contradictions in downloaded transcripts. Dataset construction is the work — it accumulates.
Buyer: Insurance defense managing partners; direct outreach via bar associations, IADC directories. Measurable ROI ($10K+ saved per matter). Willingness to pay is high in litigation; no procurement gate at mid-market firms.
Verdict: 🟢 — The expert-witness opinion graph accumulates.
Crowding: Casetext (acquired by Thomson Reuters) covers case law search but not private depositions. NewCase has 251 listed competitors broadly, but top named ones like Guidewire and Duck Creek are insurtech platforms, not deposition-level litigation tools. The private-deposition-cross-reference niche is nearly empty. FurtherAI and a few others touch adjacent insurance claims automation but don't do expert witness contradiction mining.
