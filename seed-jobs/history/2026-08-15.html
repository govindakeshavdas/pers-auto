1. Structured AI  🟡  Computer-vision QA/QC for construction drawings
Construction AI · New York · Founded 2025 · Seed (~$5M) · ~10 people · (YC F25 — included for strength of signal)
getstructured.ai · YC profile

What they do
  • Scans engineering drawing sets (MEP, structural, civil) with custom CV models.
  • Checks for code compliance, tag inconsistencies, and cross-discipline clashes.
  • Integrates with Revit; moving toward in-software "spell check" for drawings.
The problem they solve
  • Senior engineers manually redline thousands of drawings — up to half their time.
  • Example: a missing fire-damper tag on an MEP sheet causes a six-figure change order in the field.
  • 70% of construction rework traces back to drawing errors caught too late.
Money
  • SaaS sold to engineering firms; already revenue-generating.
  • $4.2M seed led by FCVC, with 20VC, Cherry, Sequoia Scout, YC.
  • Syska Hennessy (major MEP firm) is both investor and dev partner for ~4 years.
Why now
  • Vision-language models now parse dense engineering symbology reliably for the first time.
  • Industry under pressure: senior engineers retiring faster than firms can hire replacements.
Bootstrap fit
  • Grader: Objective — mechanical mismatch between drawing marks and code is measurable.
  • LLM deleted: Custom CV models remain; the value is the vision pipeline, not an LLM call.
  • Accumulates: Proprietary CV training data on engineering drawings; grows with each firm onboarded.
  • Week one: Upload a PDF drawing set → get a list of flagged inconsistencies. No historical data needed.
  • Buyer: Engineering firm principals. Reachable, but sales cycle is relationship-heavy in AEC.
Verdict: 🟡 — Real CV depth and objective grading. But: construction buyers are slow-cycle and relationship-gated. A bootstrapper without AEC network will stall on distribution. Syska partnership (4 years to build) is the moat — not replicable solo.

Crowding
  • PlanGrid (Autodesk), Newforma, Bluebeam do doc management but not AI QA/QC.
  • Few direct competitors: some ConTech startups (Togal.AI, Buildots) touch adjacent problems, not the same niche.
