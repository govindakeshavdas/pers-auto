1. Fabraix 🟡 — AI red-teaming agent that hacks customer-facing AI agents
AI security · San Francisco · founded 2025 · seed (YC S26) · ~3 people
fabraix.com · YC profile
(YC S26 — included because non-YC equivalents in automated AI red-teaming at this stage did not surface)
What they do
  • Builds AI red-teaming agents that find security holes in customer-facing AI.
  • Nyx tests agents indirectly — places malicious payloads in replicas of SaaS products, websites, documents, files, messages, and tool outputs.
  • Draws on 10,000+ collected jailbreaks as starting points for attacks.
  • Also built ACE (Adversarial Cost to Exploit), a benchmark that measures AI security in dollar cost to break a system.
The problem they solve
  • AI agents change more often than teams can test them — a new model, prompt, or tool changes behavior even when code stays the same.
  • Fully automated, so companies can retest on every change at far lower cost than a 7-figure red-team engagement.
  • Example: an enterprise deploys a customer-support agent. Nyx probes it with injected payloads and reports which ones cause data leaks — within hours, not weeks.
AgentHarm benchmark    Nyx     GPT-5.6 Sol
Attack success rate    78%     67%
Money
  • Revenue model: likely per-scan or subscription; replaces expensive annual pen tests.
  • Already found vulnerabilities in agents at dozens of Fortune 500 companies.
  • YC S26 batch. Funding amount not disclosed beyond standard YC deal.
Why now
  • AI is increasing how much software companies produce and how often it changes. Manual red-teaming can't keep pace.
  • Agentic AI with tool-use and real-world permissions created attack surfaces that didn't exist pre-2025.
Bootstrap fit
  • Grader: Objective — payload triggers or it doesn't; ACE benchmark is public and measurable.
  • LLM deleted: Jailbreak library (10K+), ACE benchmark, replica test environments remain. Technical harness.
  • Accumulates: Jailbreak corpus and attack success data grow with every scan.
  • Week one: Customer points Nyx at their agent; first vulnerability report in hours.
  • Buyer: Security teams at companies deploying AI agents. Willingness to pay is high. But: often procurement-gated at F500.
Verdict: 🟡 — Real accumulating harness (jailbreak corpus + ACE). Erodes: F500 buyer is procurement-gated, and model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) are shipping their own safety evals that could absorb this use case at the API level.
Crowding
  • XBOW raised $120M Series C for autonomous offensive security at $1B+ valuation.
  • ZioSec, General Analysis, Veria Labs all in AI agent red-teaming. Heavy seed-stage formation.
  • 16 of 27 AI safety deals in last 12 months were seed rounds.
