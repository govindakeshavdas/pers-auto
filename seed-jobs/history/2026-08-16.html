1. Caddi 🔴 — Record-to-code back-office automation for law firms and RIAs
trycaddi.com · Seattle · founded ~2024 · seed ($5M) · ~10 people · Not YC (AI2 Incubator)

What they do
  • Watches you work via screen recording, then automates the workflow.
  • Converts recordings into deterministic API-based code, not brittle RPA clicks.
  • Works across 70+ apps — DMS, CRM, email, billing.
  • Production automations for client intake, document filing, time entry, billing.
The problem they solve
  • Law firm ops staff re-key data between 5+ tools for every new client matter.
  • Classic RPA breaks on UI changes; DIY connectors need clean inputs and a team.
  • Example: read inbound contract email → extract metadata → kick off Ironclad workflow.
Money
  • Raised $5M seed led by Ubiquity Ventures, joined by Founders' Co-op and AI2 Incubator.
  • Plans to offer platform as a subscription service.
  • Positioned as "done-for-you" — part software, part managed service.
Why now
  • LLMs can now interpret screen recordings and produce code, not just replay clicks.
  • API coverage across legal/finance SaaS tools hit critical mass in 2024–25.
Bootstrap fit
  • Grader: Workflow correctness is mechanically testable (data arrives in right field).
  • LLM deleted: Left with API orchestration engine and recording parser. Technical system.
  • Accumulates: Library of law-firm workflow patterns — but erodes as computer use models improve. 🔴
  • Week one: Customer records one workflow; Caddi converts and runs it. Works day one.
  • Buyer: Ops managers at mid-size law firms. Reachable at ILTACON, Future Proof.
Verdict: 🔴 — Gate 3. UiPath, Automation Anywhere, and OpenAI's Operator all pursue the same "watch and automate" paradigm. The recording-to-API-code approach is real but converging from multiple well-funded directions. Platform absorbs.

Crowding
  • Zapier, Make for no-code; UiPath, Automation Anywhere for RPA. All competing.
  • OpenAI Operator and Anthropic computer use heading same direction from above.
