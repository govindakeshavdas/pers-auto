1. Alkali 🟡 — AI steel takeoff from PDF bid sets
steel estimation · Houston, TX / SF · founded 2025 · pre-seed (YC X25) · ~2 people · alkali.engineering · YC page

What they do
  • CV + VLMs read structural-steel drawings from PDF bid sets.
  • Auto-detect beams, columns, joists, bracing, base plates.
  • Calculate tonnage using baked-in AISC manual.
  • Convert takeoff to stock-aware nesting plans to cut waste.
The problem they solve
  • Steel fabricators estimate multimillion-dollar projects by reviewing paper blueprints with a highlighter.
  • Each month, steel fabricators use Alkali to estimate hundreds of projects; ~10% of U.S. structural steel flows through the platform.
  • Example: upload a 200-page bid set → columns and beams auto-identified and sized → tonnage and nesting plan exported to Tekla/Excel.
Money
  • SaaS subscription for fabrication shops (pricing not public).
  • Users took off more than 25,000 tons in April 2026.
  • 2 employees. YC X25 batch — (YC-backed, included for lack of non-YC equivalent).
Why now
  • VLMs (GPT-4o, Gemini) now read engineering drawings well enough for structured extraction.
  • Steel estimators are scarce; demand for faster bid turnaround is real.
Bootstrap fit
  • Grader: Tonnage/sizing from AISC standard — objective, mechanical.
  • LLM deleted: CV extraction pipeline + nesting optimizer remain. Technical system.
  • Accumulates: Trained vision model on steel drawings, but VLMs commoditize this fast.
  • Week one: Upload a bid-set PDF, get takeoff and tonnage immediately. Works cold.
  • Buyer: Estimators at steel fab shops. Reachable, pay for speed. Not procurement-gated.
Verdict: 🟡 — Real technical depth in the extraction pipeline, but large VLM providers will absorb engineering-drawing understanding as a capability. Same shape as landscaping takeoffs: genuine work, but the moat erodes as vision models improve. Also: an established steel estimation firm (e.g., Steel Estimating Solutions) hiring one ML engineer has training data + domain + customers on day one.

Crowding
  • Steel Estimating Solutions offers AI-assisted estimation; traditional tools like Tekla and Bluebeam are adjacent.
  • Space is early but multiple CV-for-construction plays are emerging (takeoff.ai, etc.).
