What they do
  • Provides math-focused RL environments and training data for frontier AI labs.
  • Supplies datasets that enable AI agents to improve their own research capabilities.
  • Maintains direct collaboration between AI researchers and mathematicians for fast iteration.
The problem they solve
  • Current data pipelines for labs take months; by arrival, model requirements have changed.
  • Example: a lab wants competition-level math problems verified by PhD mathematicians within hours, not via outsourced annotator farms.

Money

  • Revenue model: contract data-supply deals with AI labs.
Why now
  • Labs racing to improve reasoning need expert-grade math data that commodity annotation can't produce.
Bootstrap fit
  • Grader: Verifiability is real — math proofs are checkable.
  • LLM deleted: Left with a mathematician recruiting network + QA pipeline. Technical, passes.
  • Accumulates: Vetted problem sets and annotator network accumulate.

Crowding

  • Environment-native startups include Mechanize, Fleet AI, HUD, and others.
