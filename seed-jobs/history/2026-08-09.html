1. Dashnode 🟢 — AI-powered CNC costing from CAD files
dashnode.ai · Bengaluru, India · 2018 · Unfunded (bootstrapped) · ~7 people
What they do
  • Upload a CAD file, get a CNC machining cost estimate in seconds.
  • Parses geometry, calculates cycle time, material cost, setup cost.
  • Also runs DFM (design-for-manufacturability) checks on the part.
  • Bulk costing for RFQs with configurable shop-floor parameters.
The problem they solve
  • A machine shop gets an RFQ with 40 parts. Today an estimator eyeballs each.
  • Time savings of up to 90%, freeing up capacity to process more enquiries.
  • Provides file upload, configurable inputs, raw material analysis, DFM analysis, and bulk costing.
Money
  • Has not raised any funding yet.
  • SaaS: free tier for startups, paid plans for shops and enterprises.
  • 7 employees as of Apr 2026.
Why now
  • As of Dec 2025, US manufacturing had 433,000 job openings.
  • AI can parse 3D geometry and compute toolpaths — not possible at speed before.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Cost = material + cycle time + setup. Math, not opinion.
LLM deleted: The CAD parser, cycle-time calculator, and DFM rules remain.
Accumulates: Shop-specific machine/tooling profiles and validated cost data.
Week one: Upload a STEP file, get a cost. No history needed.
Buyer: Machine shop estimators and sourcing leads. Clear willingness to pay.
Verdict: 🟢 — the thing that accumulates is the validated cost model per shop config.
Crowding
  • Top competitors include Paperless Parts, 3D Spark, DigiFabster.
  • Dashnode is bootstrapped from India — lean cost base, real product.
2. Assemble Labs 🟢 — AI that runs spec-driven experiments on hardware labs

  • Menlo Ventures has invested.
  • 2 total employees.
Why now

Bootstrap fit

LLM deleted: The instrument-control harness and spec parser remain.
Accumulates: Validated test-case library per board/chip family.
Week one: Customer uploads a spec doc and connects instruments. Tests run immediately.

Verdict: 🟢 — the test harness + instrument integration is the moat.
