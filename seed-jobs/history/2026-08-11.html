1. RefortifAI 🟡 — DRM and IP protection for distributed AI models
refortif.ai · San Francisco · 2026 · YC P26 · ~3 people (YC P26 — included for lack of non-YC equivalent)
What they do
  • Obfuscate AI model weights so they run only inside a hardened runtime.
  • Drop-in replacement for vLLM, SGLang.
  • Ships models to edge/on-prem without exposing weights.
The problem they solve
  • Company fine-tunes Llama for medical imaging, deploys to hospital GPU.
  • Without protection, weights can be copied and resold.
  • RefortifAI wraps them; extraction fails.
Money
  • Likely per-deployment or SaaS license. No public revenue yet.
Why now
  • Open-weight models are fine-tuned and deployed on-prem at scale for the first time.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Weights run only in the runtime or they don't. Binary test.
LLM deleted: Crypto/obfuscation runtime remains. No LLM involved.
Accumulates: Obfuscation techniques, inference-engine compatibility.
Week one: Customer wraps model and deploys. Immediate.
Buyer: AI teams deploying models off-cloud. Reachable but niche.
Verdict: 🟡 — Real depth. Erodes: hardware TEEs and inference-engine providers could bundle. Market narrow — most providers serve via API.
Crowding
  • Fortanix, Opaque Systems — hardware-enclave approaches.
  • No direct software DRM-for-weights competitor visible yet.
