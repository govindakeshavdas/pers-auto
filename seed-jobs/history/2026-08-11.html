1. Rankwise 🟡 — Monitors whether AI search engines cite your brand, then ships the content that earns citations
tryrankwise.com · Remote · ~2025 · Bootstrapped (Founder Ventures studio) · ~2–4 people
What they do
  • Measures whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite you.
  • Finds questions AI engines answer without mentioning your brand.
  • Generates structured, schema-marked content to close the citation gap.
  • Auto-publishes articles with FAQ, Article, and Speakable schema markup.
The problem they solve
  • Scans your domain and competitor gaps to surface a prioritized queue of articles, scored by search volume, keyword difficulty, and funnel stage.
  • Every article ships with FAQ, Article, and Speakable schema — the markup that makes content visible in AI Overviews and Perplexity, not just traditional search.
  • Example: a B2B SaaS company discovers Claude answers "best expense tracker for startups" and never cites them. Rankwise generates and publishes the structured page that earns the citation.
Money
  • SaaS: tiered plans for solo founders, marketing teams, and agencies.
  • Bootstrapped through Founder Ventures studio — no disclosed raise.
  • Part of Founder Ventures, a venture studio building bootstrapped B2B SaaS, described as "the infrastructure layer for AI search."
Why now
  • Search traffic is dropping as ~60% of Google searches don't lead to clicks — users read the AI summary instead.
  • GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a new category — no entrenched incumbents yet.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Citation presence is binary — you're cited or you're not. Measurable.
LLM deleted: The monitoring harness and schema templates remain. Some value is content gen — thin.
Accumulates: Citation-tracking index across queries, engines, and time. Real dataset.
Week one: Customer enters domain, sees which AI queries miss them. No data needed from me.
Buyer: Marketing leads at B2B SaaS. Mid willingness to pay. Reachable via content channels.
Verdict: 🟡 — Monitoring + schema harness is real, but the content-gen half is a thin layer over an LLM call. Profound ($155M raised) and Promptwatch (€6M seed) are well-funded entrants. The moat erodes as AI search APIs stabilize.
Crowding
  • Profound has raised $155M across 4 rounds and is the category leader.
  • Promptwatch (Amsterdam) raised €6M seed. Bluefish AI raised $5M seed.
  • Category is forming fast — 6+ funded players in GEO/AI search optimization already.
