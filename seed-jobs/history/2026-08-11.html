1. Serafis 🟡 — Podcast knowledge graph for investors
serafis.ai · San Francisco · 2025 · Seed · ~2 people · (YC S25 — flagged, no non-YC equivalent in podcast intelligence)
What they do
  • Index 100K+ podcast transcripts into a structured knowledge graph.
  • Search by person, company, or theme — returns timestamped citations.
  • API, MCP agent tools, Snowflake data share for programmatic access.
The problem they solve
  • Info is scattered across thousands of hours of audio. Serafis builds a queryable knowledge graph — starting with podcasts.
  • Analyst needs every CEO comment on margins — buried across 40 episodes.
  • Already live with 12 organizations managing over $70B in capital.
Money
  • SaaS tiers: search, alerts, API, data warehouse feed.
  • No disclosed revenue.
Why now
  • Whisper-class ASR + entity resolution at scale became cheap in 2024–25.
  • Investors share perspectives on independent channels now, not just filings.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Timestamped transcript excerpts, not opinions. ✅
LLM deleted: Corpus + entity graph + search index remain. ✅
Accumulates: Corpus grows daily; entity graph deepens with each transcript. ✅
Week one: Customer searches a 100K-transcript library already built. ✅
Buyer: Asset managers — reachable, but AlphaSense ($3B+) already owns them. 🟡
Verdict: 🟡 — corpus accumulates; eroding factor is AlphaSense expanding into podcasts.
Crowding
  • AlphaSense overlaps on transcript discovery; Sentieo on filings search.
  • Podcast-only structured search is nearly empty as a sub-niche today.
2. Forgepoint (forgepoint.ai) 🔴 — AI inside-sales agents for US manufacturers
forgepoint.ai · Remote (US) · 2025 · Pre-seed (a16z Speedrun) · ~3 people
What they do
  • Deploy AI workers for sales, quoting, order intake, purchasing, AR, and customer service.
  • Agent reads inbound RFQ emails, drawings, specs.
  • Extracts material, tolerance, qty — applies the shop's own costing rules.
  • Outputs a reviewable quote.
The problem they solve
  • Reads fastener RFQs, pulls size, grade, thread, finish, qty, doc requirements, checks stock, prepares quote.
  • Claims 91% faster quote turnaround and 40+ hours saved per rep monthly. Go-live in <30 days.
Money
  • Pricing not disclosed. Likely per-agent SaaS.
  • Backed by a16z Speedrun, plus angels from Brex, Xwing, Betterment.
  • Claims more customer demand than they can serve.
Why now
  • LLMs reliably extract structured data from messy PDFs and emails now.
  • Reshoring pressure creates volume at shops with no back-office staff.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Quote from deterministic inputs (material + time + markup). ✅
LLM deleted: Rules engine + ERP connector remain, but thin. 🟡
Accumulates: Extraction pipelines commoditize; nothing unique accrues. 🔴
Week one: Needs ERP integration — go-live is weeks, not minutes. 🟡
Buyer: Small manufacturer owners — reachable, willing to pay. ✅
Verdict: 🔴 — Gate 3. Toolpath ($20M), Paperless Parts, Uptool, AMFG already here. Extraction pipelines don't accumulate a moat a bootstrapper can defend.
Crowding
  • Toolpath has raised $20M to date from Kennametal, Autodesk, ModuleWorks.
  • Paperless Parts, Uptool, AMFG, Jiga also in manufacturing quoting AI.
