1. Bucket Robotics 🟡 — CAD → synthetic data → defect detection for factories
bucket.bot · SF / Pittsburgh · 2024 · Seed · ~5 people · (YC S24 — no non-YC equiv found)
What they do
  • CV inspection for manufacturing — no labeling, no long pilots, no fragile rules.
  • Generate photorealistic training datasets directly from CAD files.
  • Model the part's geometry and failure modes. Working model before a camera is installed.
The problem they solve
  • Quality inspection is hard to deploy, hard to scale, and too dependent on manual labor. Most systems need months of labeling.
  • Upload CAD of injection-molded part → synthetic defect images → model on line camera, same week.
Money
  • Team from Michelin, Uber ATG, Argo AI, Stack AV. Enterprise auto/industrial customers.
  • ~$500K raised. 5 people.
Why now
  • $700B US manufacturing automation push. Quality still a trust problem.
  • Synthetic data gen now good enough to replace real-image labeling for industrial CV.
Bootstrap fit
Objective: Defect = mismatch vs. CAD geometry. Mechanically measurable.
LLM deleted: Synthetic data pipeline + CV models remain. Not an LLM wrapper.
Accumulates: Each part → synthetic dataset → trained model. Library compounds.
Week one: Customer uploads CAD, gets working inspection model pre-install.
Buyer: Mfg quality engineers. Reachable at trade shows. Pay to cut scrap.
Verdict: 🟡 — All five gates pass technically. Downgraded: enterprise mfg sales are slow for a bootstrapper, on-site deployment adds services load, YC + AV-pedigree team is a poor competitive target.
Crowding
  • Landing AI, Instrumental, Eigen — funded, some Series B+.
  • Bucket ranks 7th among 12 competitors, 3 funded. Synthetic-from-CAD is uncommon.
