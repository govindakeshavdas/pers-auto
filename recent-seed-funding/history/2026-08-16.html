2   Proxy Foods AI   🟡   AI formulation platform for food & beverage R&D
F&B / CPG · Washington, DC · $6M seed (Aug 2026)
What they do
  • AI predicts formulation performance before lab testing.
  • Ingests a company's locked-away legacy R&D files as training data.
  • Uses Expert Guided Bayesian Optimization for sensory-match predictions.
  • Claims 80% fewer development iterations; prototype timelines from months to days.
The problem they solve
  • CPG food R&D teams run hundreds of physical trials to reformulate a single product.
  • Each trial costs materials, lab time, and sensory panels.
  • Example: Barilla reformulating a pasta sauce for lower sodium needs 50+ bench trials; Proxy cuts it to <10.
Fit
  • Taste: Borderline — formulation is science (nutrition, viscosity, cost), but "taste" is literally taste. They use Bayesian models, not gut feel. Pass.
  • Can I start? Yes — USDA food-composition DB is public. Build a constraint solver in week one that optimizes ingredient ratios for nutrition and cost. Demo with open recipes.
  • Moat: Ingredient-interaction dataset that accumulates with every customer engagement. Proprietary sensory-match models. Not a single LLM call.
Verdict: 🟡 — passes, but big CPGs (Givaudan, IFF) have in-house AI R&D labs and could build this; your dataset is small vs. theirs until you sign many customers.
Crowding
  • Tastewise (consumer-trends AI, not formulation), NotCo (proprietary, not SaaS).
  • No direct US SaaS competitor doing B2B formulation prediction at this stage.
3   Relu   🟡   AI-automated design for dental lab cases
Dental / medtech · Leuven, BE + Boston, MA · $1.75M seed (Aug 2026)
What they do
  • AI designs crowns, night guards, surgical guides from CBCT scans — no human CAD technician.
  • Pay-per-case SaaS; 1M+ cases/year across 82 countries.
  • CBCT segmentation takes <15 min vs. 2–4 hrs manually.
  • CE-certified and FDA 510(k) cleared.
The problem they solve
  • Dental labs face severe technician shortages; CAD design is the bottleneck.
  • Manual crown design takes 20–40 min per case; Relu automates the common shapes.
  • Example: a lab doing 200 night guards/week saves ~80 technician-hours by sending files to Relu.
Fit
  • Taste: Pass — 3D geometry, not aesthetic judgment.
  • Can I start? Yes — public dental CBCT datasets exist (e.g., OSF repos). Train a 3D tooth-segmentation model week one. The geometry is open; the scan format is standard DICOM.
  • Moat: Model trained on 1M+ real cases. CE + FDA clearance is a 12–18 month regulatory wall. Partner API (Relu Engine) embedded in Planmeca, Ray, X-Nav.
Verdict: 🟡 — real tech and regulatory moat, but 3Shape and Align Technology have massive R&D budgets and could integrate similar AI into their existing CAD suites.
Crowding
  • 3Shape (market leader, adding AI), Overjet (dental AI for diagnostics, not lab design), DIBS AI (orthodontic planning).
  • No direct US-only competitor doing automated lab case design at this scale.
4   Zerolook   🟡   Predictive flight-shopping API for AI-scale search
Travel tech · Zug, Switzerland · €1.6M pre-seed (Aug 2026)
What they do
  • ML model predicts flight prices instead of querying airline reservation systems live.
  • REST and MCP API — plugs into ChatGPT, Claude, and travel agent runtimes.
  • Airlines pay GDS fees on every search; Zerolook replaces that cost on non-booking queries.
The problem they solve
  • AI travel agents create look-to-book ratios of 1:200,000 — searching costs more than it earns.
  • GDS queries cost $0.50–$4 each; most never convert to a booking.
  • Example: an OTA serving Claude-powered trip planning burns $200K/month on GDS fees for searches that never book.
Fit
  • Taste: Pass — price prediction is math, not judgment.
  • Can I start? Yes — scrape public flight prices (Google Flights, Skyscanner). Train a price-prediction model on historical route/fare data. Ship an API in week one.
  • Moat: Fare-prediction model gets better with every API call's feedback loop. Accuracy is measurable; customers compare against live GDS. The training dataset is the accumulating asset.
Verdict: 🟡 — real tech, but Google (ITA/QPX) and Amadeus can add predictive caching to their own stacks. Your moat erodes if a GDS offers the same thing natively.
Crowding
  • Amadeus, Travelport, Duffel — all provide live results, none do predictive/cached results yet.
  • Google Flights (ITA Software) is the latent threat — owns the data already.
