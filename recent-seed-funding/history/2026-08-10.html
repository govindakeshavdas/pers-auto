  • Ingest entire transaction data rooms for apartment deals.
  • Classify and analyze thousands of files in minutes.
  • Output structured, source-linked data for lease audits.
  • Cover rent rolls, vendor contracts, utility agreements, financials.
The problem they solve
  • Multifamily due diligence takes weeks of manual spreadsheet work.
  • Teams of 10+ people review 15–30 files per resident by hand.
  • Example: Preiss needed 15–20 people working round the clock for three days when a deal fell through and came back with 30% turnover. Rely did the same audit in under an hour.

Money

  • Led by 2048 Ventures. Range Ventures, Better Tomorrow Ventures.
Moat
For:
  • Document formats in multifamily are chaotic — scanned leases, photographed addenda.

Against:
  • Real estate firms are notoriously slow to adopt new tech.
  • Deal flow is cyclical. Revenue could be lumpy.
  • General-purpose doc AI (like Google Vertex) could eat this from below.
Verdict: 🟢 Tight vertical. Source-tracing is a real wedge.
Crowding
  • No well-funded direct competitor doing AI multifamily diligence at seed+.
