**This is a discovery pass only.** Collect names. Do not evaluate them, do not verify them, do not write analysis. A second pass does all of that.

**How to search.** Cast wide. Most good deals never get press, so lean on general searches rather than the big outlets. Vary the phrasing each run — combine round language ("raises $3M seed", "closes pre-seed", "led the round", "emerges from stealth with funding") with AI product terms (AI agent, AI automation, AI infrastructure, applied AI) and whatever verticals you are rotating through this run. Run these across Google, smaller funding newsletters, regional tech press, investor announcement posts, and company blogs.

**Three hard requirements. A candidate that fails any one is worthless downstream — drop it.**

1. **A real, named company.** You must be able to name the actual company. Never emit "Stealth AI Startup", "Unnamed healthcare AI", or a label you invented. A script matches these names against every company sent in previous runs, so an invented label defeats that check and the same company resurfaces every run. A company emerging from stealth *with* a name is fine.
2. **A real, recent round.** You must have seen an actual announcement of a pre-seed or seed round closed in the last 2 months. Portfolio pages, "top AI startups" lists, accelerator batch pages, and company indexes are **not** funding signals — they list companies regardless of any round. Use them at most to recognise a name you found in a real announcement. No announcement, no candidate.
3. **A URL that points at that announcement or the company.** A funding post, press item, investor announcement, or the company site. Never a search-results page and never a directory or list page. The next pass has to verify from this link.

Return up to 50 candidates. Over-collect: a later pass shortlists them and only then researches the survivors, so a weak candidate costs almost nothing while a missed one is gone for the run. But volume never justifies breaking the three requirements — 20 companies with real announcements beat 50 scraped off a portfolio page. Companies from previous runs are removed by script before the next pass sees them.

**Output format.** One line per company and nothing else:

Use the plain company name only — this is parsed by a script.
